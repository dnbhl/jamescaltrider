About

Musician.

Drummer.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Percussion Performance from Keene State College, where I also picked up sticks at 12. Since then — four years drumming on cruise ships, stages across NYC including Brooklyn Bowl, Mercury Lounge, and Bowery Ballroom, and sessions with artists across every genre.

As a teacher, I combine technical depth with real performance experience. We work on what you actually want to play. I figure out your goals, your learning style, and what keeps you excited — then build from there.

FAQs

Common
questions.

What skill levels do you teach?

All levels welcome — complete beginners through advanced drummers looking to sharpen technique, expand musicality, or take on new styles.

Do I need a drum set to start?

No. Beginners can start with sticks and a practice pad. Electric kits are great for NYC apartments — quiet, compact, and versatile. I'm happy to recommend one.

What styles do you specialize in?

Rock, jazz, funk, hip-hop, blues, pop, and more. Every lesson is shaped around your taste — we work on what excites you.

How does a typical lesson work?

We warm up together, then work on patterns, grooves, fills, or full songs. I use an iPad for visual references to help ideas land faster.

What do you charge?

Reach out for current rates. First trial lesson is discounted for new students — no commitment required.

What's your teaching style?

Musical, tailored, and actually fun. Whether we're jamming to songs you love or breaking down complex grooves step by step — it should feel worth your time.
